🚀 Free-Body Diagram Lab

A free-body diagram (force diagram) shows every force acting on one object as arrows. In this lab, forces point only up, down, left or right — no diagonals!

Part 1 · Draw it (10 pts)Read the condition of the object, then draw its free-body diagram. 5 questions × 2 pts.
Part 2 · Write it (20 pts)Look at a given free-body diagram and write a simple equation for the forces on one axis. 10 questions × 2 pts.
Use any inputMouse, pen, touch screen or keyboard. Sounds and a read-aloud button (🗣) are on — use 🔊 to mute.

Rules for this lab: g = 10 m/s². Right (+x) and up (+y) are positive. Forces: Fg gravity, N normal, Fa applied push/pull, Ff friction, T tension.

Your name:

1) Pick a force 2) Drag from the center of the object

⌨ Keyboard: 1–5 pick a force · arrow keys draw (press again to make it longer, Shift+arrow shorter) · Delete removes · Enter checks. Arrow length follows the scale bar: 15 px = 5 N.
Tap the tiles (or type with the keyboard). Use 0 when forces are balanced and ma when the object is speeding up.
